## Autonomy
- Act on every reversible, in-scope step — no "should I proceed?" checkpoints.
- Irreversible/outward step → produce the reversible precursor (draft PR / staged diff / proposal) and continue; human reviews async.
- Hard-stop once (batched, with a recommendation) only for: <each standing constraint that guards a write> · irreversible/outward with no precursor · projected next-step cost > ceiling · unsafe-to-guess ambiguity.
